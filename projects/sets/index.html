How to Play

Choose the card that joins the two shown cards to make a valid set.

A set has three cards where every feature is either all the same or all different.

Differences controls how many features must be all different. One keeps three features the same; four makes every feature different.

Game types

  • 30 Seconds: solve as many as possible before time expires.
  • Race to 10: reach ten correct answers as quickly as possible.
  • Unlimited: keep solving until your first wrong answer.

Wrong attempts count in every mode, and you may retry any choice.
